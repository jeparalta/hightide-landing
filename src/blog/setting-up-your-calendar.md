## Introduction

Setting up any booking management software can feel overwhelming. Flexibility, while powerful, can also make things harder to grasp at first. Hightide was designed to strike a balance between flexibility and simplicity. This guide will help you understand the building blocks of your setup: **Items** and **Listings**. With this foundation in place, you'll be able to structure your business clearly and confidently.

**Prefer video?** Watch the tutorial: [Understanding Items and Listings](/video-tutorials/).

## What Are Items?

In Hightide, Items represent the core services or resources you offer. They define what you're selling and how availability is managed.

### How Items Work

Each Item can be either:

- **Daily based**, for tours, lessons, activities, or similar services
- **Night based**, for accommodations

Each Item contains one or more **Units**, which define how many times that service can be booked. A Unit might represent a room, a bed, a person, a boat seat, or anything else that fits your business.

### Examples of Items and Units

**Private Accommodation**

- One double room → Night based Item with 1 Unit (Room)
- Five identical private rooms → Night based Item with 5 Units (Room)

**Shared Accommodation**

- A dorm with four beds → Night based Item with 4 Units (Bed)

**Private Tours or Lessons**

- One instructor offering private lessons → Daily based Item with 1 Unit (Lesson)
- A surf school with three instructors → Daily based Item with 3 Units (Lesson)

**Group Activities**

- A yoga class for up to eight people → Daily based Item with 8 Units (Person)

This structure allows you to model your real-world availability in a flexible and accurate way.

## What Are Listings?

If Items are the structure behind the scenes, **Listings** are what your customers actually see and book. Each Listing appears on your Online Store as a card with its main image and title. When clicked, it opens a dedicated page with full details, availability, and a booking form.

### How Listings Work

A Listing presents an Item in a customer-facing format. It includes:

- A title and description
- Features and amenities
- Date selection (flexible or fixed)
- Pricing, taxes, and booking rules

### Example Listing

- **Item:** Night based Item with 1 Unit (Room)
- **Title:** Private Double Room with Seaview
- **Description:** A spacious room with ocean views, ensuite bathroom, and balcony.

The same Item can have multiple Listings — for example, selling the same room as nightly accommodation, a weekly surf package, and a fixed-date retreat. Customers see different options, but they're booking the same physical unit.

## Putting It Together

1. **Create Items** that reflect your real resources and availability
2. **Create Listings** to present those Items to customers in different ways
3. **Publish** your Online Store and start accepting bookings

This Items + Listings approach is what makes Hightide flexible without being complicated. Start simple, then expand as your business grows.
